How Bitcoin BIP110 was created.

On 8 August 2026, the mandatory enforcement of BIP 110 on the Bitcoin BTC mainnet caused a chain split at block height 961,632. The new chain became Bitcoin BIP110.

Wallet safety

Safety considerations after the chain split.

Ordinary transactions can remain valid across compatible histories. The proposed SIGHASH_UNIFIED design is opt-in, signature-specific, and active only in documented RC2 testnet code as of this snapshot.

Do not move inherited coins without understanding replay behavior and verifying the exact software and rules active on both chains.
