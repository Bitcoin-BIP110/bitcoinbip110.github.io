PSA-260902 · September 2, 2026 Network is Live

Bitcoin BIP110 mainnet is live with its first production release.

Bitcoin BIP110 has completed its transition to BLAKE2b proof of work and reached its first production mainnet release.

The live chain transitioned from SHA-256d to BLAKE2b proof of work at block 961,640 on August 30, 2026. Bitcoin Knots v29.4.1.knots20260508 was then released on September 2, 2026 as the first final production release carrying the Bitcoin BIP110 mainnet consensus changes.

Mainnet status: Block 961,639 is the final SHA-256d block on this branch. Block 961,640 is the first BLAKE2b block and was mined on August 30, 2026 at 06:14:39 UTC.

The final release retains the live chain established during the release-candidate process. There was no mainnet reset between RC5 and the production release. Nodes following the BLAKE2b chain therefore continue from the same block history beginning at height 961,640.

  • Bitcoin BIP110 now uses BLAKE2b proof of work and version-2 block headers from height 961,640 forward.
  • The release includes the reduced-data flag-day changes and temporary 800 kWU block-weight limit.
  • Opt-in SIGHASH_UNIFIED support is included for chain-specific transaction signing.
  • Nodes, miners, wallets, explorers, exchanges, and other infrastructure must support the Bitcoin BIP110 consensus rules to operate on the current network.

Running Bitcoin BIP110

Node operators should use the current Bitcoin BIP110 release and verify downloads before installing or upgrading. The Software page contains the current release, release notes, checksums, signatures, source code, and supporting resources.

The Node's Guide covers installation, configuration, verification, upgrading, and operation of a Bitcoin BIP110 node. Existing operators should confirm that their node is following the BLAKE2b chain from block 961,640.

Mining Bitcoin BIP110

Bitcoin BIP110 can no longer be mined with SHA-256d Bitcoin ASICs. Miners must use BLAKE2b-compatible hardware together with software capable of constructing and submitting Bitcoin BIP110 blocks. The Miner's Guide covers the current mining workflow and network requirements.

Verify the chain: The current Bitcoin BIP110 network should show block 961,639 as the final SHA-256d block and block 961,640 as the first BLAKE2b block. Network status can be independently checked using compatible nodes and explorers such as mempool.guide.

PSA-260901 · September 1, 2026 Public Safety Warning

Neoxa Exchange / neoxa.exchange is a high-risk exchange claiming to offer Bitcoin BIP110.

BitcoinBIP110.org strongly recommends that users avoid dealing with Neoxa Exchange until they have been varified.

We currently consider Neoxa Exchange a high-risk, unverified custodial exchange claiming to offer Bitcoin BIP110 trading under a false name and ticker. We recommend that users avoid depositing funds.

Important: Multiple reports of users depositing funds and not being able to withdraw.

  • Article: NeoxEX Exchange Scam Costs Users Thousands
  • Reddit: User's detailed experience of having their funds locked.
  • X.com: User unable to withdraw funds.
  • The exchange is claiming to list Bitcoin BIP110 under a false name and ticker.
  • Withdrawals are manually reviewed. Neoxa Exchange itself states that every withdrawal is subject to manual review.
  • The exchange is labeled BETA. Neoxa describes the trading platform as still being actively developed and polished.
  • The exchange has limited independent operating history and third-party reputation.
  • Neoxa operates the exchange and related infrastructure itself, concentrating custody and counterparty risk within the same organization.
  • We have not identified publicly available proof of reserves and liabilities or an independent security audit covering the exchange's custody system.
  • The Bitcoin BIP110 market being circulated appears extremely thin and should not be treated as reliable price discovery without independently verifiable liquidity, deposits, withdrawals, and trading activity.

BitcoinBIP110.org has documented Bitcoin-holder safety as a primary concern surrounding this chain split. Fraudulent wallets, claiming services, misleading exchanges, phishing sites, and other schemes are expected risks whenever holders believe they may control coins on a newly separated chain.

Influencers: Please Stop Amplifying the Unverified Exchanges and Unverified Prices Related To Bitcoin BIP110.

If you have posted or quoted a Bitcoin BIP110 price from Neoxa Exchange, please issue a correction or safety notice and warn your audience to proceed with caution before directing funds toward this platform.

A number displayed on an exchange interface does not establish a trustworthy market price.

Protect Your Bitcoin

Never enter a Bitcoin seed phrase or private key into a claiming website.

Do not move valuable Bitcoin BTC solely to obtain Bitcoin BIP110.

Do not assume that an exchange listing, ticker, screenshot, or displayed price is legitimate because it is being repeated by influencers or social-media accounts.

Important: Do not send Bitcoin to a website simply because it claims to let you buy, sell, split, or claim Bitcoin BIP110.
